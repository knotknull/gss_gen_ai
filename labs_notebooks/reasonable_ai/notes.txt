Google's ethical aims 
Objectives for AI Applications:

- Be socially beneficial
    - do not unfaily deny essential services (housig, insurance, education) 
    - reduce uninteded harms

- Avoid creating or reinforcing unfair bias
    - limit influence of historical bias
    - lifecycle of data (sample, label, train) can introduce bias
    - Ask questions:
        - Who are intended users 
        - what other groups may be impacted
        - what groups are invisible today
        - how was training data collected, sampled and labeled
        - is training data skewed
        - how was model tested and validated
        - is model behaving (operating ?) as exptected

- built and tested for safety
    - promote safety, bodily integrity, overall heal of people, communities, properties and systems
    - oversight of testing
    
- Be accounttable to people
    - respect people's rights and independence
    - limiiting lack of opt-out
    - informed consent
    
- incorporate privacy design principles
    - protect privacy / safety of individuals and groups
    - personally identifiable / sensitive data handled carefully with robust security 
    
- uphold high standards of scientific excellence
    - follow rigorous scientific approaches  
    - integrity / collaboration / inquiry
    
- be made available for uses that accord with these principles (??)
    - how close a technology can be used for a harmful use

# Lessons Learned: Best Practices

1. Benefit in assembling review committee: 
    - diverse in identity 
    - expertise
    - seniority
    - include DEI

2. Top down / bottom up support and engagement

3. Train teams (product and tech) on tech ethics
   - encourage non-tech stakeholders to develop an understanding of 
      tech, business and societal impacts of AI

4. Align goals and motivations of business and responsible AI team align 
   - responsible AI == successful AI

5. Transparency in AI governance process

6. System to track alignment of plans, including:
   - issues, mitigations, precedents
     
7. Take a humble approach
     
8. Invest in psychological safety
    - explore potential issues
    - BUT ground issue spotting in technical, business and societal realities

9. Efficiency is not the primary goal  
    - balance between dev goals and comprehensive review

10. Assume each AI app needs attention / review
